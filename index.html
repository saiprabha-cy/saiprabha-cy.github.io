ABOUT

Engineering focus

My engineering work focuses on embedded systems, firmware, avionics and aerospace software, with emphasis on understanding the complete path from system requirements to implementation and verification.

My projects combine software implementation with system modelling, communication protocols, spacecraft simulation, telemetry and engineering documentation.

The goal is not only to make a system run, but to understand its architecture, interfaces, assumptions, failure modes and validation evidence.
